\begin{definition}[Infinity-harmonic function]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $\Omega\subseteq E$
and let $u:E\to\R$. We say $u$ is \emph{infinity-harmonic on $\Omega$} if $u$ is
both an infinity subsolution on $\Omega$ in the sense of
\noderef{IsInfSubsolutionOn} and an infinity supersolution on $\Omega$ in the
sense of \noderef{IsInfSupersolutionOn}. Explicitly: $\Omega$ is open, $u$ is
continuous on $\Omega$, every local upper $C^2$ test $\phi$ for $u$ at
$z\in\Omega$ satisfies $\Dinf\phi(z)\ge0$, and every local lower test satisfies
$\Dinf\phi(z)\le0$.
\end{definition}
