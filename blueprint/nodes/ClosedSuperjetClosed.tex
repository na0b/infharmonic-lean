\begin{lemma}[The closed superjet is a closed set]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $f:E\to\R$ and let
$z\in E$. Then the closed superjet $\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$ of
\noderef{ClosedSuperjet} is a closed subset of $E\times\mathcal B(E)$, where
$\mathcal B(E)$ denotes the space of continuous bilinear forms on $E$ with the
operator norm.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since $E\times\mathcal B(E)$ is a metric space, it suffices to show that
$\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$ contains the limit of every convergent sequence of its
elements.

So let $(p^{(m)},X^{(m)})\in\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$ for $m\ge1$ with
$p^{(m)}\to p$ in $E$ and $X^{(m)}\to X$ in $\mathcal B(E)$; we must show
$(p,X)\in\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$.

\emph{A diagonal choice.}
Fix $m\ge1$. By \noderef{ClosedSuperjet} applied to
$(p^{(m)},X^{(m)})\in\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$ there are sequences
$(z^{(m)}_k)_k$, $(p^{(m)}_k)_k$, $(X^{(m)}_k)_k$ with
\[
  (p^{(m)}_k,X^{(m)}_k)\in J^{2,+}f\bigl(z^{(m)}_k\bigr)\quad(k\ge0),
\]
and $z^{(m)}_k\to z$, $f(z^{(m)}_k)\to f(z)$, $p^{(m)}_k\to p^{(m)}$ and
$X^{(m)}_k\to X^{(m)}$ as $k\to\infty$, where $J^{2,+}$ is the superjet of
\noderef{Superjet}. Because these four sequences converge to the displayed
limits, and $1/m>0$, we may choose an index $k(m)$ so large that all four of
\[
  \bigl|z^{(m)}_{k(m)}-z\bigr|<\tfrac1m,\quad
  \bigl|f(z^{(m)}_{k(m)})-f(z)\bigr|<\tfrac1m,\quad
  \bigl|p^{(m)}_{k(m)}-p^{(m)}\bigr|<\tfrac1m,\quad
  \bigl\|X^{(m)}_{k(m)}-X^{(m)}\bigr\|<\tfrac1m
\]
hold simultaneously: each of the four conditions holds for all sufficiently
large $k$, so any $k(m)$ beyond the largest of the four thresholds works.

Put $\zeta_m=z^{(m)}_{k(m)}$, $\pi_m=p^{(m)}_{k(m)}$ and
$\Xi_m=X^{(m)}_{k(m)}$.

\emph{The diagonal sequences have the required properties.}
First, $(\pi_m,\Xi_m)\in J^{2,+}f(\zeta_m)$ for every $m$, by the displayed
membership with $k=k(m)$.

Second, $|\zeta_m-z|<1/m\to0$, so $\zeta_m\to z$.

Third, $|f(\zeta_m)-f(z)|<1/m\to0$, so $f(\zeta_m)\to f(z)$.

Fourth, $|\pi_m-p|\le|\pi_m-p^{(m)}|+|p^{(m)}-p|<\tfrac1m+|p^{(m)}-p|$, and both
terms tend to $0$ (the second because $p^{(m)}\to p$), so $\pi_m\to p$.

Fifth, and in the same way,
$\|\Xi_m-X\|\le\|\Xi_m-X^{(m)}\|+\|X^{(m)}-X\|<\tfrac1m+\|X^{(m)}-X\|\to0$, so
$\Xi_m\to X$.

These five statements are precisely the requirements in the definition of
\noderef{ClosedSuperjet} for the pair $(p,X)$ relative to $f$ at $z$, with
defining sequences $(\zeta_m,\pi_m,\Xi_m)_m$. Hence
$(p,X)\in\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$, and $\overline J^{2,+}f(z)$ is closed.
\end{proof}
