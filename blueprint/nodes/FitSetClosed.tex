\begin{helper}[Fits of a fixed radius form a closed set]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $C_0\in\R$, let
$v$ be a continuous real function on space-time and let $r>0$. Then the set of
triples $(z,p,a)$ in $(F\times\R)\times F\times\R$ such that $(p,a)$ is a fit
for $v$ at $z$ of radius $r$ with constant $C_0$, in the sense of
\noderef{IsFit}, is closed.
\end{helper}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{IsFit}, $(p,a)$ is a fit for $v$ at $z$ of radius $r$ with constant
$C_0$ if and only if
\begin{equation}\label{eq:fsc-fit}
  \Bigl|v\bigl(z+(h,\ell)\bigr)-v(z)-\langle p,h\rangle
     -\bigl(a-\tfrac12|p|^2\bigr)\ell\Bigr|\le C_0r
\end{equation}
for every increment $(h,\ell)$ with $|\ell|\le r$ and $|h-\ell p|\le r$. The
constraint on the increments involves $p$, so the domain of the quantified
condition varies with the point of the set under consideration. We remove that
dependence by reparameterizing the increments: for $\theta\in\R$ and $\zeta\in F$
put
\[
  \ell(\theta)=r\theta,\qquad h(\theta,\zeta,p)=r\theta\,p+r\zeta .
\]

\emph{The reparameterization is a bijection onto the admissible increments.}
Fix $(z,p,a)$. If $|\theta|\le1$ and $|\zeta|\le1$ then
$|\ell(\theta)|=r|\theta|\le r$ and
$|h(\theta,\zeta,p)-\ell(\theta)p|=|r\zeta|=r|\zeta|\le r$, so
$(h(\theta,\zeta,p),\ell(\theta))$ is an admissible increment. Conversely, if
$(h,\ell)$ satisfies $|\ell|\le r$ and $|h-\ell p|\le r$, put
$\theta=\ell/r$ and $\zeta=r^{-1}(h-\ell p)$, which is legitimate because
$r>0$; then $|\theta|=|\ell|/r\le1$ and $|\zeta|=|h-\ell p|/r\le1$, while
$\ell(\theta)=r(\ell/r)=\ell$ and
$h(\theta,\zeta,p)=\ell p+(h-\ell p)=h$. Hence, for every $(z,p,a)$, the
condition of \eqref{eq:fsc-fit} over all admissible increments is equivalent to
the same condition over the increments $(h(\theta,\zeta,p),\ell(\theta))$ with
$|\theta|\le1$ and $|\zeta|\le1$.

\emph{Each reparameterized condition is closed.} Fix $\theta$ with
$|\theta|\le1$ and $\zeta$ with $|\zeta|\le1$, and consider
\[
  S_{\theta,\zeta}=\Bigl\{(z,p,a):\bigl|v\bigl(z+(r\theta p+r\zeta,\,r\theta)\bigr)
    -v(z)-\langle p,\,r\theta p+r\zeta\rangle
    -\bigl(a-\tfrac12|p|^2\bigr)r\theta\bigr|\le C_0r\Bigr\}.
\]
The map
$(z,p,a)\mapsto v(z+(r\theta p+r\zeta,r\theta))-v(z)-\langle p,r\theta p+r\zeta\rangle
 -(a-\tfrac12|p|^2)r\theta$
is continuous, being built from the continuous $v$, the continuous vector space
operations, the continuous inner product and the continuous norm, with
$\theta$, $\zeta$ and $r$ fixed; composing with the continuous absolute value
and taking the preimage of the closed half-line $(-\infty,C_0r]$ shows that
$S_{\theta,\zeta}$ is closed.

\emph{Conclusion.} By the previous two paragraphs the set in question is
\[
  \bigcap_{|\theta|\le1}\ \bigcap_{|\zeta|\le1}S_{\theta,\zeta},
\]
an intersection of closed sets, hence closed. \end{proof}
