\begin{definition}[Oscillation]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $u:E\to\R$ and let
$S\subseteq E$. The \emph{oscillation of $u$ on $S$} is
\[
  \osc_Su=\sup_Su-\inf_Su
    =\sup\,u(S)-\inf\,u(S).
\]
Here $\sup u(S)$ and $\inf u(S)$ use the convention of the preamble, so that a
statement controlling $\osc_Su$ is meaningful only in the presence of the
matching boundedness hypotheses on $u(S)$, which are always stated explicitly.
\end{definition}
