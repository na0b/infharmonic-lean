\begin{definition}[Root mean square radius of a configuration]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $n$ be a
nonnegative integer, let $\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n\in\R$, let
$y_1,\ldots,y_n\in F$ and let $x\in F$. The \emph{root mean square radius about
$x$} is
\[
  r=\left(\sum_{i=1}^n\theta_i|y_i-x|^2\right)^{1/2}.
\]
When $\theta_i>0$, $\sum_i\theta_i=1$ and $\sum_i\theta_iy_i=x$, this is the
radius of that configuration, and $\sum_i\theta_i|y_i|^2-|x|^2=r^2$.
\end{definition}
