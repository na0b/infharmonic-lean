\begin{definition}[Supersolution of the limiting equation]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $\Omega$ be a
subset of the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$ and let $v$ be a real function on
space-time. We say $v$ is a \emph{viscosity supersolution of $F[v]=0$ on
$\Omega$} if $\Omega$ is open, $v$ is continuous on $\Omega$, and for every
$z\in\Omega$ and every $\phi$ that is $C^2$ near $z$ such that $w\mapsto
v(w)-\phi(w)$ has a local minimum on $\Omega$ at $z$ one has
\[
  F[\phi](z)\le0,
\]
with $F$ as in \noderef{LimitOp}. Tests involve all variables $(x,t)$ together,
and all tests are admitted.
\end{definition}
