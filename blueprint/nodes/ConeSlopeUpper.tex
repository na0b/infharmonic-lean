\begin{definition}[Upper cone slope]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $w:E\to\R$, let
$z\in E$ and let $r\in\R$. The \emph{upper cone slope of $w$ at $z$ of radius
$r$} is
\[
  L_r^+(w;z)=\sup_{|h|\le r}\frac{w(z+h)-w(z)}{r},
\]
the supremum being over the closed ball $\{h\in E:|h|\le r\}$. For $r>0$ and $w$
continuous on a neighbourhood of $\overline B_r(z)$ this supremum is a maximum
and equals $r^{-1}\max_{|h|\le r}\{w(z+h)-w(z)\}$.
\end{definition}
