\begin{definition}[Envelope Hamiltonian]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $f$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$ and let $z$ be a space-time
point. The \emph{envelope Hamiltonian} is the extended real number
\[
  H_f(z)=\inf_{\ell>0}\ \Senv_\ell^+f(z)\ \in[-\infty,+\infty),
\]
the infimum in $\overline\R$ of the forward envelope slopes of
\noderef{EnvSlopeP} over all positive lengths. The value is never $+\infty$
because each slope is a real number. When $\ell\mapsto\Senv_\ell^+f(z)$ is
nondecreasing, this infimum is exactly the limit
$\lim_{\ell\downarrow0}\Senv_\ell^+f(z)$.
\end{definition}
