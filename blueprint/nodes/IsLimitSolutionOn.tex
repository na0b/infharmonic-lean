\begin{definition}[Solution of the limiting equation]
Let $F$, $\Omega$ and $v$ be as in \noderef{IsLimitSubOn}. We say $v$ is a
\emph{viscosity solution of}
\[
  F[v]:=D^2v[(\nabla_xv,1)]=0
\]
\emph{on $\Omega$} if $v$ is both a viscosity subsolution of $F[v]=0$ on
$\Omega$ in the sense of \noderef{IsLimitSubOn} and a viscosity supersolution of
$F[v]=0$ on $\Omega$ in the sense of \noderef{IsLimitSuperOn}.
\end{definition}
