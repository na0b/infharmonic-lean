\begin{definition}[Affine function on space-time]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let $v$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$. We say $v$ is \emph{affine} if
there are $q\in F$ and $b,c\in\R$ with
\[
  v(x,t)=q\cdot x+bt+c\qquad\text{for all }(x,t).
\]
\end{definition}
