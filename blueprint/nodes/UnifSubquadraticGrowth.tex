\begin{definition}[Uniform subquadratic spatial growth for a sequence]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $I\subseteq\R$ and
let $f_1,f_2,\ldots$ be real functions on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$. We
say the sequence has \emph{uniform subquadratic spatial growth on compact time
intervals of $I$} if for every compact $J\subseteq I$
\[
  \lim_{M\to\infty}\ \sup_j\ \sup_{\substack{|y|\ge M\\ t\in J}}
      \frac{|f_j(y,t)|}{|y|^2}=0 .
\]
Explicitly: for every compact $J\subseteq I$ and every $\varepsilon>0$ there is
$M>0$ such that $|f_j(y,t)|\le\varepsilon|y|^2$ for every index $j$ and whenever
$|y|\ge M$ and $t\in J$.
\end{definition}
