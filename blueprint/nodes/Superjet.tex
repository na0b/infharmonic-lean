\begin{definition}[Second-order superjet]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $g:E\to\R$ and let
$z\in E$. The \emph{second-order superjet} $J^{2,+}g(z)$ is the set of pairs
$(p,X)$, with $p\in E$ and $X$ a symmetric continuous bilinear form on $E$, such
that
\[
  g(z+h)\le g(z)+p\cdot h+\tfrac12X[h]+o(|h|^2)
  \qquad(h\to0),
\]
where $X[h]=X(h,h)$. Explicitly: for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$
with
\[
  g(z+h)\le g(z)+\langle p,h\rangle+\tfrac12X(h,h)+\varepsilon|h|^2
  \qquad(|h|<\delta).
\]
\end{definition}
