\begin{definition}[Optimizing step pair]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $v$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$, let $\kappa,\epsilon\in\R$ and
let $z=(x,t)$, $z^+$, $z^-$ be space-time points. Write
$W=\Cenv_tv-\kappa^2t/2$ for the contact envelope of \noderef{ContactEnv} and
$f=W$. We say $(z^+,z^-)$ is an \emph{optimizing step pair of size $\epsilon$ at
$z$} if there are $Y^+,Y^-\in F$ such that
\begin{gather*}
  \Penv_{2\epsilon}f(z)
    =f(Y^+,t+2\epsilon)-\frac{|Y^+-x|^2}{2\cdot2\epsilon},
  \qquad
  \Menv_{2\epsilon}v(z)
    =v(Y^-,t-2\epsilon)+\frac{|Y^--x|^2}{2\cdot2\epsilon},
  \\
  z^+=\left(\frac{x+Y^+}{2},\,t+\epsilon\right),
  \qquad
  z^-=\left(\frac{x+Y^-}{2},\,t-\epsilon\right),
\end{gather*}
with $\Penv$, $\Menv$ as in \noderef{QuadEnvP} and \noderef{QuadEnvM}. Thus
$Y^+$ attains the forward envelope of $f$ at $z$ at length $2\epsilon$, $Y^-$
attains the backward envelope of $v$ at $z$ at length $2\epsilon$, and
$z^\pm$ are the intermediate points at times $t\pm\epsilon$ on the straight
paths from $x$ to those optimizers. The associated step velocities are
\[
  Q_+=\frac{x^+-x}{\epsilon},\qquad
  Q_-=\frac{x-x^-}{\epsilon},
\]
where $z^\pm=(x^\pm,t\pm\epsilon)$; both are oriented forward in time.
\end{definition}
