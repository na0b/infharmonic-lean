\begin{definition}[Closed superjet]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $g:E\to\R$ and let
$z\in E$. The \emph{closed superjet} $\overline J^{2,+}g(z)$ is the set of pairs
$(p,X)$, with $p\in E$ and $X$ a continuous bilinear form on $E$, for
which there are sequences $z_k\in E$, $p_k\in E$ and symmetric continuous
bilinear forms $X_k$ with
\[
  (p_k,X_k)\in J^{2,+}g(z_k)\quad\text{for all }k,\qquad
  z_k\to z,\quad g(z_k)\to g(z),\quad p_k\to p,\quad X_k\to X,
\]
where $J^{2,+}$ is the superjet of \noderef{Superjet}. Thus closed superjets are
limits of superjets at points approaching $z$, with the function values also
converging; the symmetry of $X$ is automatic from the symmetry of the $X_k$.
\end{definition}
