\begin{definition}[Sheared change of frame]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $v$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$, let $z_0=(x_0,t_0)$ be a
space-time point, let $R\in\R$, let $p_0\in F$ and let $a_0\in\R$. The
\emph{change of frame of $v$ based at $z_0$ with scale $R$ and parameters
$(p_0,a_0)$} is the function
\[
  \bar v(y,s)=
  \frac{v\bigl(x_0+R(y+p_0s),\,t_0+Rs\bigr)-v(x_0,t_0)}{R}
       -p_0\cdot y-\left(a_0+\frac{|p_0|^2}{2}\right)s .
\]
It sends $z_0$ to the space-time origin, where its value is $0$.
\end{definition}
