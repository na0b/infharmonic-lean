\begin{definition}[The class $\Aclass_{m,C_0}$]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let $C_0\in\R$. A
real function $v$ on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$ belongs to the class
$\Aclass_{m,C_0}$, with $m=\dim F$, if
\begin{enumerate}
\item $v$ is continuous on all of space-time and is a viscosity solution of
$F[v]=0$ there, in the sense of \noderef{IsLimitSolutionOn} with
$\Omega$ the whole space; and
\item for every space-time center $z$ and every $R>0$ there are $p\in F$ and
$a\in\R$ such that $(p,a)$ is a fit for $v$ at $z$ of radius $R$ with constant
$C_0$ in the sense of \noderef{IsFit}.
\end{enumerate}
The fit parameters are allowed to depend on both the center and the radius.
\end{definition}
