\begin{definition}[Forward quadratic envelope]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $f$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$, let $\ell\in\R$ and let
$z=(x,t)$ be a space-time point. The \emph{forward quadratic envelope} is
\[
  \Penv_\ell f(x,t)
    =\sup_{y\in F}\left\{f(y,t+\ell)-\frac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}\right\}
  \qquad(\ell\ne0),
  \qquad
  \Penv_0f=f .
\]
By the convention of the preamble the supremum for $\ell\ne0$ is meaningful only
together with the hypotheses guaranteeing that the set of competitor values is
bounded above, which are always stated explicitly.
\end{definition}
