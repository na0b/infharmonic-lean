\begin{definition}[Hölder seminorm]
Let $E$, $V$, $g$, $\alpha$ and $S$ be as in \noderef{HolderQuotSet}. The
\emph{H\"older seminorm of $g$ on $S$ at exponent $\alpha$} is
\[
  [g]_{C^{0,\alpha}(S)}
   =\sup Q_\alpha(g;S)
   =\sup_{\substack{x,y\in S\\x\ne y}}
       \frac{|g(x)-g(y)|}{|x-y|^\alpha},
\]
the supremum of the set of H\"older quotients of \noderef{HolderQuotSet}. By the
convention of the preamble this supremum is meaningful only together with the
hypothesis that $Q_\alpha(g;S)$ is bounded above, which is always stated
explicitly.
\end{definition}
