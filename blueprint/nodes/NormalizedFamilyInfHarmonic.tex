\begin{lemma}[Members of the normalized family are infinity-harmonic on the unit ball]
Fix an integer $d\ge0$, write $\R^d$ for the Euclidean space of dimension $d$ and
identify space-time with $\ell^2(\R^d\times\R)$, and let $B_1$ denote the open
unit ball about the origin of space-time. Let
$U:\ell^2(\R^d\times\R)\to\R$ belong to the normalized lifted family in the sense
of \noderef{NormalizedInfFamily}. Then $U$ is infinity-harmonic on $B_1$ in the
sense of \noderef{IsInfHarmonicOn}.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
By \noderef{NormalizedInfFamily} there is a function $u:\R^d\to\R$, among whose
listed properties are that $u$ is infinity-harmonic on the open unit ball
$B_1^d$ of $\R^d$ in the sense of \noderef{IsInfHarmonicOn} and that
\begin{equation}\label{eq:nfih-lift}
  U(z)=u(z_x)+z_t\qquad\bigl(z=(z_x,z_t)\in\ell^2(\R^d\times\R)\bigr).
\end{equation}
(The remaining properties listed there --- $u(0)=0$, the boundedness of the image
of $B_1^d$ above and below, and the bound on the oscillation --- are not used
here.)

\emph{The lift is infinity-harmonic on the slab.} Applying \noderef{LinearLift}
to the open set $B_1^d$ and the function $u$, the function
$z\mapsto u(z_x)+z_t$ is infinity-harmonic, in the sense of
\noderef{IsInfHarmonicOn}, on the preimage of $B_1^d$ under the projection
$z\mapsto z_x$, that is, on the slab
\[
  S=\bigl\{z\in\ell^2(\R^d\times\R):|z_x|<1\bigr\}.
\]
By \eqref{eq:nfih-lift} that function is exactly $U$, so $U$ is
infinity-harmonic on $S$.

\emph{The unit ball of space-time lies in the slab.} The $\ell^2$ norm of
space-time satisfies $|z|^2=|z_x|^2+|z_t|^2$, so $|z_x|^2\le|z|^2$ and hence
$|z_x|\le|z|$, both being nonnegative. Therefore $|z|<1$ implies $|z_x|<1$, i.e.
\[
  B_1\subseteq S .
\]

\emph{Restriction.} The ball $B_1$ is open, it is contained in $S$, and $U$ is
infinity-harmonic on $S$; so \noderef{InfHarmonicMono} applies and gives that
$U$ is infinity-harmonic on $B_1$ in the sense of \noderef{IsInfHarmonicOn}.
\end{proof}
