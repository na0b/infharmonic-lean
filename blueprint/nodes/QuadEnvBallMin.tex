\begin{lemma}[The backward envelope as a minimum over a fixed ball]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $f$ be a real
function on space-time, let $\ell>0$, let $z=(x,t)$ be a space-time point and let
$M\in\R$ satisfy $|x|\le M$. Write $B_M=\{y\in F:|y|\le M\}$ and assume
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] $y\mapsto f(y,t-\ell)$ is continuous on $B_M$;
\item[(b)] $f(x,t-\ell)<f(y,t-\ell)+\dfrac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}$ for every $y\in F$
with $|y|>M$.
\end{enumerate}
Then the backward quadratic envelope of \noderef{QuadEnvM} satisfies
\[
  \Menv_\ell f(z)
  =\inf\Bigl\{f(y,t-\ell)+\frac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}\ :\ y\in B_M\Bigr\},
\]
and this value is attained in $B_M$: there is $y^\ast\in F$ with $|y^\ast|\le M$
and
\[
  \Menv_\ell f(z)=f(y^\ast,t-\ell)+\frac{|y^\ast-x|^2}{2\ell}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Write
\[
  G(y)=f(y,t-\ell)+\frac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}\qquad(y\in F),
\]
so that, by \noderef{QuadEnvM} and $\ell\ne0$, the envelope $\Menv_\ell f(z)$ is
the infimum of the competitor set $\mathcal V=\{G(y):y\in F\}$, while the infimum
in the assertion is that of $\mathcal V_M=\{G(y):y\in B_M\}$.

\emph{A minimizer on the ball.}
$B_M$ is a closed bounded subset of the finite-dimensional space $F$, hence
compact, and it is nonempty because $|x|\le M$ gives $x\in B_M$. The function
$y\mapsto|y-x|^2/(2\ell)$ is continuous on $F$, since $\ell>0$ makes the
denominator a nonzero constant; together with (a) this makes $G$ continuous on
$B_M$. A continuous real function on a nonempty compact set attains its minimum,
so there is $y^\ast\in B_M$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qebmin-min}
  G(y^\ast)\le G(y)\qquad(y\in B_M).
\end{equation}
In particular $\mathcal V_M$ is nonempty and bounded below by $G(y^\ast)$, and
$G(y^\ast)\in\mathcal V_M$, so $G(y^\ast)$ is the least element of $\mathcal V_M$
and $\inf\mathcal V_M=G(y^\ast)$. Here the infimum is a genuine greatest lower
bound and not the value $0$ that the convention of the preamble assigns to an
empty or unbounded-below set, precisely because nonemptiness and the lower bound
$G(y^\ast)$ have just been exhibited.

\emph{The ball is not restrictive.}
Since $x\in B_M$ and $|x-x|=0$, \eqref{eq:qebmin-min} at $y=x$ gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:qebmin-center}
  G(y^\ast)\le G(x)=f(x,t-\ell).
\end{equation}
Let $y\in F$ be arbitrary. If $|y|\le M$ then $G(y^\ast)\le G(y)$ by
\eqref{eq:qebmin-min}. If $|y|>M$ then (b) and \eqref{eq:qebmin-center} give
\[
  G(y^\ast)\le f(x,t-\ell)<G(y).
\]
Hence $G(y^\ast)\le G(y)$ for every $y\in F$, i.e. $\mathcal V$ is bounded below
by $G(y^\ast)$; and $\mathcal V$ is nonempty, containing $G(x)$. Since also
$G(y^\ast)\in\mathcal V$, the number $G(y^\ast)$ is the least element of
$\mathcal V$, so $\inf\mathcal V=G(y^\ast)$; again the boundedness and
nonemptiness needed for the preamble's convention not to intervene have been
exhibited.

\emph{Conclusion.}
By \noderef{QuadEnvM}, $\Menv_\ell f(z)=\inf\mathcal V=G(y^\ast)
=\inf\mathcal V_M$, which is the displayed identity, and the witness
$y^\ast\in B_M$, i.e. $|y^\ast|\le M$, satisfies
$\Menv_\ell f(z)=G(y^\ast)=f(y^\ast,t-\ell)+|y^\ast-x|^2/(2\ell)$, which is the
attainment statement.
\end{proof}
