\begin{lemma}[The stopped walks and their expectation bounds]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let
$1\le C_0<\infty$, let $\kappa>0$ and $H\in\R$, and let $v\in\Aclass_{m,C_0}$
in the sense of \noderef{IsFitClass} be centered at $z_*=(0,1)$ with constant
$C_0$ in the sense of \noderef{IsCenteredAt} and satisfy the horizontal Jensen
bound with constant $\kappa$ at every time in the sense of
\noderef{HasJensenBound}. Let $W$ be the contact envelope of
\noderef{ContactEnv} formed from $v$ and $\kappa$, and assume the standing
properties of the extremal contact pair:
\begin{enumerate}
\item $W$ is continuous on $\{(x,t):t>0\}$ and has subquadratic spatial growth
on compact subintervals of $(0,\infty)$ in the sense of
\noderef{SubquadraticGrowth};
\item $W$ is a viscosity subsolution of the limiting equation on
$\{(x,t):t>0\}$ in the sense of \noderef{IsLimitSubOn};
\item $v(x,t)-\kappa^2t/2\le W(x,t)\le v(x,t)$ for $t>0$, and
$W(z_*)=v(z_*)=0$;
\item $x\mapsto W(x,t)-|x|^2/(2t)$ is concave on $F$ for every $t>0$;
\item if $t>0$ and $W(x,t)=v(x,t)$ then $(\Cenv_tv(x,t)-v(x,t))/t=\kappa^2/2$,
with $\Cenv$ the barycentric envelope of \noderef{BaryEnv};
\item the envelope Hamiltonian of \noderef{EnvHamiltonian} satisfies
$H_v(z_*)=H$, and the forward envelope slopes of \noderef{EnvSlopeP} satisfy
$\Senv_\ell^+W(z_*)\to H$ as $\ell\downarrow0$.
\end{enumerate}
Write $J_\epsilon$ for the quantity of \noderef{JGap}, $\delta_j=2^{-j}$ and
$L(K)=1+\log K$.

Then there is a constant $C_*>0$ such that for every integer $T\ge2$, every
$R\ge2$ and every integer $N\ge1$ there are $\epsilon>0$, a measure space
$(\Omega,\mathcal M,\mu)$ with a filtration $(\mathcal G_n)$, functions
$\xi_n$, $z_n$, $z^+_n$, $z^-_n$, $Q_0(n)$ and $\tau$ which form a stopped
contact walk for $(\kappa,H,T,R,v,N,\epsilon)$ in the sense of
\noderef{IsContactWalk}, such that, writing
\[
  Q_+(n)=\frac{x^+_n-x_n}{\epsilon},\qquad
  Q_-(n)=\frac{x_n-x^-_n}{\epsilon},\qquad
  \mathcal E_n=|Q_+(n)-Q_0(n)|^2+|Q_-(n)-Q_0(n)|^2,
\]
the following two bounds hold with $B=R+T+2$:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] for every stopping time $\sigma$ for $(\mathcal G_n)$ with
$\sigma\le\tau$ pointwise, the function $J_\epsilon(z_\sigma)$ is
$\mu$-integrable and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contact-stopped-expectation}
  \mathbb E J_\epsilon(z_\sigma)\ge J_\epsilon(z_*);
\end{equation}
\item[(ii)] the function $\sum_{n<\tau}\mathcal E_n$ is $\mu$-integrable and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:contact-error-budget}
  \mathbb E\sum_{n<\tau}\mathcal E_n\le B_0,\qquad B_0=C_*L(B)^4 ;
\end{equation}
\item[(iii)] there are marks with fits $(\sigma_j,p_j,a_j)$ for
$(C_0,v,N,\mathcal G,z,\tau)$ in the sense of \noderef{IsMarkData}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Hypotheses (1) and (6) and the normalizations in (3) are the only parts of the
standing package used below; hypotheses (2), (4), (5) and the sandwich
inequalities of (3) are not needed for this assembly.

\emph{The constant.} By \noderef{ContactWalkBudgets}, applied to $C_0$, $\kappa$,
$H$ and $v$ --- its hypotheses are that $v$ lies in $\Aclass_{m,C_0}$ in the
sense of \noderef{IsFitClass}, is centered at $z_*$ with constant $C_0$ in the
sense of \noderef{IsCenteredAt} and satisfies the horizontal Jensen bound with
constant $\kappa$ in the sense of \noderef{HasJensenBound}, together with
hypothesis (1) for the contact envelope $W$ of \noderef{ContactEnv} --- there is
a constant $C_*>0$ with the property stated there. That node produces $C_*$
before $T$, $R$ and $N$ are quantified, so the single $C_*$ serves every box;
this is the uniformity the present statement asserts. Fix now an integer
$T\ge2$, a real $R\ge2$ and an integer $N\ge1$.

\emph{The walk.} By \noderef{ContactWalkExists}, applied with these $T$, $R$ and
$N$ --- its hypotheses are the same three assumptions on $v$, hypothesis (1) for
$W$, the normalizations $W(z_*)=0$ and $v(z_*)=0$ from hypothesis (3), and
hypothesis (6), namely $H_v(z_*)=H$ for the envelope Hamiltonian of
\noderef{EnvHamiltonian} and $\Senv^+_\ell W(z_*)\to H$ for the forward envelope
slopes of \noderef{EnvSlopeP} --- there are $\epsilon>0$, a measure space
$(\Omega,\mathcal M,\mu)$ with a filtration $(\mathcal G_n)$, and functions
$\xi_n$, $z_n$, $z^+_n$, $z^-_n$, $Q_0(n)$ and $\tau$ on it which form a stopped
contact walk for $(\kappa,H,T,R,v,N,\epsilon)$ in the sense of
\noderef{IsContactWalk}, and such that the range of each $z_n$ is at most
countable.

\emph{Conclusions (i) and (ii).} \noderef{ContactWalkBudgets} quantifies the
walk universally: its conclusion applies to every $\epsilon$ and every stopped
contact walk for $(\kappa,H,T,R,v,N,\epsilon)$, in particular to the one just
produced. Its two conclusions at this $T$, $R$, $N$, $\epsilon$ and this walk are
verbatim conclusions (i) and (ii) of the present statement, with the same
constant $C_*$ and the same $B=R+T+2$.

\emph{Conclusion (iii).} By \noderef{ContactWalkMarkData}, applied to the same
walk --- its hypotheses are that $v$ lies in $\Aclass_{m,C_0}$, that the data
form a stopped contact walk in the sense of \noderef{IsContactWalk}, and that the
range of each $z_n$ is at most countable, the last being the final assertion of
\noderef{ContactWalkExists} --- there are marks with fits
$(\sigma_j,p_j,a_j)$ for $(C_0,v,N,\mathcal G,z,\tau)$ in the sense of
\noderef{IsMarkData}. This is conclusion (iii), and the lemma follows with the
constant $C_*$ fixed above. \end{proof}
