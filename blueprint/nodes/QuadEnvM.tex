\begin{definition}[Backward quadratic envelope]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $f$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$, let $\ell\in\R$ and let
$z=(x,t)$ be a space-time point. The \emph{backward quadratic envelope} is
\[
  \Menv_\ell f(x,t)
    =\inf_{y\in F}\left\{f(y,t-\ell)+\frac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}\right\}
  \qquad(\ell\ne0),
  \qquad
  \Menv_0f=f .
\]
By the convention of the preamble the infimum for $\ell\ne0$ is meaningful only
together with the hypotheses guaranteeing that the set of competitor values is
bounded below, which are always stated explicitly.
\end{definition}
