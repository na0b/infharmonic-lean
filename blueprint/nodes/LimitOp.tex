\begin{definition}[The limiting operator]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let
$\phi:F\times\R\to\R$ be defined on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$ of the
preamble. For a space-time point $z$ set
\[
  F[\phi](z)=D^2\phi(z)\bigl[(\nabla_x\phi(z),1)\bigr],
\]
the value of the second derivative of $\phi$ at $z$ on the pair
$(\xi,\xi)$ with $\xi=(\nabla_x\phi(z),1)$, where $\nabla_x\phi(z)\in F$ is the
spatial part of the full space-time gradient $\nabla\phi(z)$. For $F=\R^m$ and
smooth $\phi$ this is $D^2\phi[(\nabla_x\phi,1)]$ in the notation
$A[\xi]=\xi^{\mathsf T}A\xi$.
\end{definition}
