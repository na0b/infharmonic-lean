\begin{definition}[Contact envelope]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $v$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$ and let $\kappa\in\R$. The
\emph{contact envelope} is
\[
  W(x,t)=\Cenv_t v(x,t)-\frac{\kappa^2t}{2},
\]
where the barycentric parameter is taken equal to the time coordinate and
$\Cenv$ is the envelope of \noderef{BaryEnv}. We write
$W=\Cenv_tv-\kappa^2t/2$; the construction is used for $t>0$.
\end{definition}
