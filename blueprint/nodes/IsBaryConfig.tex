\begin{definition}[Finite horizontal probability configuration]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $n$ be a
nonnegative integer, let $\theta_1,\ldots,\theta_n\in\R$, let
$y_1,\ldots,y_n\in F$ and let $x\in F$. We say $(\theta,y)$ is a \emph{finite
horizontal probability configuration with mean $x$} if
\[
  \theta_i>0\ (1\le i\le n),\qquad \sum_{i=1}^n\theta_i=1,\qquad
  \sum_{i=1}^n\theta_iy_i=x .
\]
The single-point configuration $n=1$, $\theta_1=1$, $y_1=x$ is always
admissible.
\end{definition}
