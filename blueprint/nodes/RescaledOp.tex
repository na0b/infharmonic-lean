\begin{definition}[The rescaled operator]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $\lambda\in\R$ and
let $\phi$ be a real function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$. For a
space-time point $z$ set
\[
  F_\lambda[\phi](z)
   =D^2\phi(z)\bigl[(\nabla_x\phi(z),\,1+\lambda\phi_t(z))\bigr],
\]
the value of the second derivative of $\phi$ at $z$ on the pair $(\xi,\xi)$ with
$\xi=(\nabla_x\phi(z),1+\lambda\phi_t(z))$, where $\nabla_x\phi(z)\in F$ and
$\phi_t(z)\in\R$ are the spatial and time parts of the space-time gradient
$\nabla\phi(z)$. For $\lambda=0$ this is the limiting operator $F[\phi]$ of
\noderef{LimitOp}.
\end{definition}
