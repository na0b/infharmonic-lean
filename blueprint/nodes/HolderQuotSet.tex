\begin{definition}[Hölder difference quotients]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $V$ be a normed
space, let $g:E\to V$, let $\alpha\in\R$ and let $S\subseteq E$. The
\emph{set of H\"older quotients of $g$ on $S$ at exponent $\alpha$} is
\[
  Q_\alpha(g;S)
   =\left\{\frac{|g(x)-g(y)|}{|x-y|^\alpha}
        \;:\;x,y\in S,\ x\ne y\right\}\subseteq\R .
\]
\end{definition}
