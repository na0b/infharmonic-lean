\begin{definition}[Time reversal]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let $f$ be a real
function on the space-time $\ell^2(F\times\R)$. Its \emph{time reversal} is
\[
  \widetilde f(x,t)=-f(x,-t).
\]
We write $\widetilde f$ for this function and $\widetilde z=(x,-t)$ for the
reflection of a space-time point $z=(x,t)$ in the spatial hyperplane, so that
$\widetilde f(z)=-f(\widetilde z)$.
\end{definition}
