\begin{lemma}[Completing the square across a two-leg quadratic cost]
Let $F$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space and let $t,\ell>0$. Then for all
$y,x\in F$, writing $s=t+\ell$,
\[
  \frac{|y|^2}{2s}-\frac{|y-x|^2}{2\ell}-\frac{|x|^2}{2t}
    =-\frac{t}{2\ell s}\Bigl|y-\frac stx\Bigr|^2 .
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Since $t>0$ and $\ell>0$ we have $s=t+\ell>0$, so each of $t$, $\ell$ and $s$ is nonzero
and every division below is legitimate.

Both sides are quadratic in $(y,x)$, so it suffices to expand each in the three
quantities $|y|^2$, $\langle y,x\rangle$ and $|x|^2$ and compare coefficients. In an inner
product space $|u-w|^2=|u|^2-2\langle u,w\rangle+|w|^2$, so
\[
  |y-x|^2=|y|^2-2\langle y,x\rangle+|x|^2,
\]
and, applying the same identity with $w=(s/t)x$ and using
$\langle y,(s/t)x\rangle=(s/t)\langle y,x\rangle$ together with
$|(s/t)x|^2=(s/t)^2|x|^2$,
\[
  \Bigl|y-\frac stx\Bigr|^2
   =|y|^2-2\,\frac st\langle y,x\rangle+\frac{s^2}{t^2}|x|^2 .
\]

Substituting these two expansions, the left-hand side becomes
\[
  \Bigl(\frac1{2s}-\frac1{2\ell}\Bigr)|y|^2
   +\frac1\ell\langle y,x\rangle
   +\Bigl(-\frac1{2\ell}-\frac1{2t}\Bigr)|x|^2 ,
\]
and the right-hand side becomes
\[
  -\frac{t}{2\ell s}|y|^2
   +\frac{t}{2\ell s}\cdot\frac{2s}{t}\langle y,x\rangle
   -\frac{t}{2\ell s}\cdot\frac{s^2}{t^2}|x|^2 .
\]
We compare the three coefficients.

For $|y|^2$: on the left $\dfrac1{2s}-\dfrac1{2\ell}=\dfrac{\ell-s}{2\ell s}
=\dfrac{-t}{2\ell s}$, using $s=t+\ell$ so that $\ell-s=-t$; this is the coefficient on
the right.

For $\langle y,x\rangle$: on the left it is $\dfrac1\ell$, and on the right
$\dfrac{t}{2\ell s}\cdot\dfrac{2s}{t}=\dfrac1\ell$.

For $|x|^2$: on the left
$-\dfrac1{2\ell}-\dfrac1{2t}=-\dfrac{t+\ell}{2\ell t}=-\dfrac{s}{2\ell t}$, again by
$s=t+\ell$; on the right
$-\dfrac{t}{2\ell s}\cdot\dfrac{s^2}{t^2}=-\dfrac{s}{2\ell t}$.

All three coefficients agree, so the two sides are equal for all $y,x\in F$.
\end{proof}
