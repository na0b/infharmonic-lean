\begin{definition}[Infinity supersolution]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $\Omega\subseteq E$
and let $u:E\to\R$. We say $u$ is an \emph{infinity supersolution on $\Omega$} if
$\Omega$ is open, $u$ is continuous on $\Omega$, and for every $z\in\Omega$ and
every $\phi:E\to\R$ that is $C^2$ near $z$ such that $x\mapsto u(x)-\phi(x)$ has
a local minimum on $\Omega$ at $z$, one has
\[
  \Dinf\phi(z)\le0 ,
\]
with $\Dinf$ as in \noderef{InfLap}. Such a $\phi$ is a \emph{local lower test
for $u$ at $z$}. All tests are admitted, including those with
$\nabla\phi(z)=0$.
\end{definition}
