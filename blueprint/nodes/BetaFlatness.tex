\begin{definition}[The bad-parameter supremum $\beta_u(z,r)$]
Let $E$ be a finite-dimensional real inner-product space, let $u:E\to\R$, let
$z\in E$ and let $r\in\R$. Put
\[
  \beta_u(z,r)=\sup\{\lambda\in(0,1):\lambda\text{ is not good for }u
                        \text{ at }(z,r)\},
\]
with goodness as in \noderef{IsCylinderFit}, and with the value $0$ when the set
is empty. Thus $0\le\beta_u(z,r)\le1$, and every parameter in
$(\beta_u(z,r),1)$ is good for $u$ at $(z,r)$.
\end{definition}
