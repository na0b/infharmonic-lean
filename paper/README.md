# The paper

`paper.tex` is the LaTeX source of

> OpenAI, *Uniform Interior C<sup>1,α</sup> Estimates for Infinity-Harmonic Functions*,
> OpenAI Math Release preprint, October 4, 2026,
> <https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Uniform-Interior-C1alpha-Estimates-for-Infinity-Harmonic-Functions-October-4-2026/interior-c1-infinity-harmonic.pdf>

in the form that was formalized. The paper is © OpenAI and is distributed in [openai/math](https://github.com/openai/math) under the Apache License 2.0; it is included here under the same license (see [`../LICENSE`](../LICENSE)).

The source was modified only in its presentation. The section files were merged into one file; the figures, the table of contents and the expository subsection "Context and methods" were removed, together with the sentences that referred to the figures; `tikz` was replaced by `xcolor`. No statement, proof or label was changed, so the numbering of theorems and equations is that of the original preprint.
